## SEGMENT 4: THE INVESTIGATION — WHAT DO WE KNOW? (3-4 min)

The hosts need to play detective here. Be critical. Be investigative. This is the segment that turns the episode from a race recap into a SCANDAL story.

**What we know:**
- The pre-race catered meal affected the majority of the field
- Drivers began showing symptoms approximately 30 minutes before the green flag
- Symptoms included nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps, and worse (multiple drivers reportedly had to deal with... digestive urgency... while strapped into a stock car at 140+ mph)
- The severity varied: some drivers (Becker, 32 incidents) were able to race through it. Others (Jordan, 60 incidents) could barely finish.
- Two drivers were completely unaffected: Nick Green and Blaine Carnes
- Both unaffected drivers say they brought their own food and didn't eat from the catered spread

**What we DON'T know:**
- Who had access to the food
- What the contamination was
- Whether it was intentional or accidental (food handling error? allergic reaction? deliberate sabotage?)
- Whether anyone outside the league had motive

**The uncomfortable questions the hosts should raise:**
1. "The two drivers who weren't sick are the two who happened to bring their own food. Is that suspicious or smart?" — One host should say it's a coincidence (plenty of athletes bring their own meals). The other should say the optics are TERRIBLE.
2. "Nick Green needed this race to go badly for Becker to win the championship. And it did." — This is not an accusation, but it's the reality. The hosts should note they are NOT accusing Nick, but the narrative writes itself.
3. "Blaine said he ALWAYS brings his own food. Justin — who runs the league — was affected. If this was targeted, Justin being affected actually helps clear Nick, because why would you poison your own league administrator?"
4. "The league MUST investigate before Stage 2 begins. There need to be protocols — sealed food, independent catering, something. This cannot happen again."
5. "Ronald Ramsey wasn't there and Ronald wasn't affected. Does that tell us anything? Or does it just mean being at a lake is healthier than being at a race track buffet?"

**The asterisk discussion:** One host should argue the Stage 1 championship stands as-is — Nick drove a legitimately improved race and you can't undo results based on off-track events. The other host should argue that the league needs to acknowledge the circumstances — maybe not void the results, but at MINIMUM launch a formal investigation and put an asterisk in the record book.
